A bit about me

I'm a UI/UX designer who believes that great design is invisible — it just works. Over the past 6 years, I've worked with startups and enterprise teams across e-commerce, fintech, healthcare, and SaaS, delivering user-centered interfaces that balance aesthetics with usability.

My process is rooted in user research and rapid iteration. I love collaborating with product managers, engineers, and stakeholders to translate ambiguous problems into clear, elegant solutions. When I'm not pushing pixels, I'm exploring typography, sketching UI concepts, or tinkering with CSS animations.
